Nyumbani Kurejesha Akaunti Kulinda Akaunti Onyo la Scams Kuhusu

Jinsi ya Kulinda Akaunti Zako

Fanya checklist hii sasa ili uzuie wizi wa akaunti.

Checklist ya Usalama (Fanya Leo)

  • Washa Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) kwenye Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok, X, Gmail, n.k.
  • Tumia password yenye nguvu na unique — angalau herufi 12, mchanganyiko wa herufi, namba na alama. Usitumie password ile ile mahali pengi.
  • Tumia Password Manager (kama Bitwarden, 1Password, au Google Password Manager) ili uweze kuweka password tofauti kwa kila akaunti.
  • Angalia Login Activity mara kwa mara (Settings → Security → Where you're logged in) na uondoe devices usizozijua.
  • Ondoa Third-Party Apps usizozitumii (Apps and Websites / Connected Apps).
  • Hakikisha email na namba ya simu kwenye akaunti ni sahihi na unazo access.
  • Usibonyeze links za ajabu kutoka email, SMS, au DM — hata kama zinaonekana kutoka Instagram/Facebook.
  • Washa alerts za login na mabadiliko ya email/password.

Jinsi ya Kuweka 2FA (Muhimu Zaidi)

Instagram / Facebook:
Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security → Two-factor authentication → Chagua Authentication App (inapendekezwa) au SMS.
WhatsApp:
Settings → Account → Two-step verification → Weka PIN ya tarakimu 6.
Gmail / Google:
myaccount.google.com → Security → 2-Step Verification.

Password Yenye Nguvu

Password nzuri:

  • Ina angalau herufi 12–16
  • Ina herufi kubwa + ndogo + namba + alama (!@#$%)
  • Sio jina lako, tarehe ya kuzaliwa, au neno rahisi
  • Tofauti kwa kila akaunti

Mfano mzuri: Ng0mbe#2026Salama! (usitumie hii, tengeneza yako)

Jinsi ya Kuepuka Phishing

  • Instagram haitakuomba password kupitia DM au email.
  • Angalia anwani ya email kwa makini (security@mail.instagram.com ni sahihi).
  • Usibonyeze link yoyote inayokuambia "akaunti yako itafungwa" au "thibitisha sasa".
  • Ikiwa unashaka, fungua app ya Instagram moja kwa moja badala ya kufuata link.
Kidokezo cha Ziada: Weka backup codes za 2FA mahali salama (sio kwenye simu ile ile). Ikiwa utapoteza simu, bado utaweza kuingia.
